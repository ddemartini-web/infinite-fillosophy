INFINITE FILLOSOPHY — AGENCY MODEL & PRICING FRAMEWORK
Working framework for website and agency materials. Proposed prices below are planning ranges, not a finalized public rate card.

POSITIONING
Infinite Fillosophy is a boutique creative agency delivering premium-level thinking through flexible creative partnerships. It can work with established companies, brands, production companies, advertising agencies, entrepreneurs and emerging businesses. Scope and team structure flex to the opportunity; the quality of thinking does not.

The agency can lead a project, work alongside a client's existing team, act as an embedded creative partner, or assemble a flexible team of trusted professionals. It sells intellectual capital, creative leadership and storytelling expertise—not simply hours.

PITCH DECK DEVELOPMENT — STANDALONE SERVICE
Clients do not need to commission a full production workup to engage Infinite Fillosophy for a pitch deck. Offer three levels:
1. Pitch Deck Design & Development: for clients with an existing idea or project who need story organization, narrative flow, written-content refinement, visual direction and a polished presentation.
2. Pitch Deck Strategy & Creation: for clients with an idea that needs concept refinement, story/audience positioning, narrative development and complete deck creation.
3. Comprehensive Development Package: for clients seeking deeper original format development, format architecture, episode structure, character/access strategy, production approach and a pitch deck, with optional sizzle/presentation strategy.

WAYS TO WORK TOGETHER

COMPLIMENTARY INTRODUCTORY CONVERSATION — 30 MINUTES
A no-obligation introductory call to understand the client's goals, explore the opportunity, assess compatibility and determine whether Infinite Fillosophy may be the right partner. It is not a working session and does not include creative development, strategic recommendations or concept brainstorming.

PAID CREATIVE STRATEGY CONSULTATION
For clients seeking professional insight, creative direction, story assessment, original format guidance or strategic recommendations. Scope, duration, fee and any deliverables are agreed in advance. The client is paying for professional expertise, not receiving ownership or unrestricted usage rights to ideas, pre-existing IP or materials. Use a written consultation agreement reviewed by an entertainment attorney.

PROJECT-BASED ENGAGEMENTS — PRIMARY PRICING MODEL
Each project is individually scoped around the brief, creative complexity, deliverables, schedule, team requirements, level of creative/production leadership, and rights/usage. Fees may be a defined project fee or phased development fee. The client receives a written proposal specifying objectives, deliverables, timeline, investment, assumptions and terms before work begins. Production costs, travel, talent, crew, equipment, post-production and third-party expenses are identified separately where applicable.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CONFIDENTIALITY
Do not imply that a consultation or project fee automatically transfers ownership of pre-existing intellectual property or grants unrestricted use of ideas and materials. Written agreements should distinguish pre-existing IP from newly created deliverables and specify ownership, licensing, permitted use, confidentiality, payment, and any option/production rights. Have an entertainment attorney prepare/review reusable agreements.
